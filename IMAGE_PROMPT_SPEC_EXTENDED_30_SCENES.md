## 優先級 TOP 10 詳細 Image Prompt

### 圖1：「鋼鐵獨舞」（操 / Steel Solitude Dance）

**建議輸出文件名**：`ConceptArt_Scene_Misao_SteelSolitude.png`

### Image Prompt

```
A haunting illustration of a young girl suspended in mid-dance within an abandoned industrial factory, 
captured in a moment of profound solitude and self-transformation. 

THE PROTAGONIST - MISAO (綾小路操):
- Age: 16 years old, inverted triangle physique (refined, dancer-like build)
- Current state: Transitioning between human and doll-like appearance
- Hair: Long black hair with single gold braid, flowing dramatically around her as if caught mid-spin
- Expression: Serene yet heartbreaking, eyes closed as if in deep meditation or acceptance
- Costume elements visible:
  - Fragments of her white magical girl suit clinging to the upper body
  - Gothic dress elements (Victorian corset suggestion) bleeding into puppet/porcelain aesthetic
  - Multiple puppet strings (invisible to normal sight) visible in magical glow, creating intricate web patterns
  - String patterns forming delicate but tragic geometric designs

VISUAL FOCUS: PUPPET STRINGS AS PRIMARY MOTIF
- Iridescent puppet threads emanating from her limbs, torso, and head
- Strings glowing with soft purple-blue ethereal light
- Each string is impossibly thin yet visually powerful, suggesting control and loss of control simultaneously
- Strings form X-patterns and spiral configurations around her body
- Some strings appear to be broken or fraying, suggesting her state of half-transformation
- The strings cast faint shadows on the surrounding space

THE STAGE - ABANDONED FACTORY SETTING:
- Vertical composition emphasizing the "suspended in space" quality
- Rusted metal framework in background, visible through haze
- Hanging chains and industrial pulleys above, creating downward visual pressure
- Broken skylights allowing cold moonlight to stream in multiple directions
- Floor obscured by shadow and mist, suggesting she floats between two worlds
- Scattered industrial debris (gears, metal plates, rivets) below, barely visible
- Temperature: cold, metallic, industrial yet somehow intimate

LIGHTING AND COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary light source: Pale moonlight (cool 4000K) filtered through broken skylights
- Magical light: Soft violet-blue glow radiating from puppet strings (#7B68EE, #6A5ACD)
- Misao's body: Pale porcelain white (#F5F5F5) with subtle shadowing
- Factory materials: Rust orange (#B7410E), steel gray (#4A4A4A), weathered brown (#6B4423)
- Atmospheric haze: Bluish-gray mist (#A9B0B8) creating depth
- Shadow dominance: 60% of image in deep shadow, 40% illuminated

SYMBOLIC VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Gold braid: Single element of warmth and rebellion against cold perfection
- Shattered mirror fragments visible among debris (reference to her identity crisis)
- Handkerchief (Sayo's memory object) hanging from one of the strings like a charm or weight
- Dance posture: Not aggressive or powerful, but vulnerable—like a doll on display

EMOTIONAL TONE AND ATMOSPHERE:
- Simultaneously beautiful and tragic
- Elegance mixed with industrial coldness
- A sense of chosen solitude rather than imposed imprisonment
- The "dance" quality suggests movement and choice, even as strings suggest control
- Melancholy acceptance: she has chosen this fate
- The beauty of breaking free and the tragedy of losing humanity in the process

COMPOSITION STRUCTURE:
- Central figure dominates (70% visual focus)
- Symmetrical arrangement of strings creates visual balance
- Depth layers: Foreground (strings/body), Midground (factory), Background (sky through skylights)
- Vertical emphasis with subtle 15-degree tilt suggesting motion
- Rule of thirds: Misao positioned slightly off-center, strings creating lead lines toward upper corners

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:
- Photorealistic rendering of factory details mixed with painterly ethereal light effects
- String threads rendered with subsurface scattering for glowing effect
- Dust particles visible in moonlight beams (volumetric lighting)
- Fabric simulation for dangling elements (handkerchief, torn dress pieces)
- Soft focus on outer edges (vignette effect) to emphasize isolation
- Canvas aspect ratio: 3:4 portrait (vertical dominance)
- Resolution quality: Professional illustration standard

REFERENCE AESTHETIC:
- Combines porcelain doll aesthetic with industrial gothic
- Inspired by ballet photography capturing mid-leap moments
- Color grading similar to: *Madoka Magica* transformation scenes crossed with *Steins;Gate* lab environments
- Emotional weight similar to: *Revolutionary Girl Utena* battle sequences

CRITICAL VISUAL MESSAGE:
- The strings are not visible chains but ethereal connections (magic, control, identity)
- She is dancing, not struggling—suggesting agency and acceptance
- The factory setting is not a prison but her chosen stage
- Beauty and tragedy are inseparable in this image
```

```
CENTER - HARUKA FORMS (晴香):

HARUKA FORM B - THE SHADOW ONE (Fei / 夕 - The Shadow Self):

- Eyes: Dark, hollow, carrying the weight of all trauma and regret
- Costume: Torn, stained version of the magical girl suit, or shadowy silhouette

- Expression: Defensive yet yearning, trying to shield while also seeking acceptance

FUSION POINT - THE CENTER:
- Where the two Harukas meet, they begin to blur and merge

  - Floating memories/scenes dissolving in and out: school uniforms, battle scars, warm meals, late-night cries

- Dreamlike atmosphere: No harsh edges, everything slightly soft-focused except the central figures
- Lighting: No single light source; illumination seems to come from within the figures themselves

- A faint mirror image behind them (suggesting the doubling of self)
- Spiraling threads or connections linking all three figures (representing the complex bonds)

The image conveys:
- "Acceptance is not the erasure of pain, but the integration of it"
- "The shadow self is not the enemy; it is the orphaned part seeking reunion"
- "Motherly love means: 'I see all of you—the bright and the broken—and I choose all of you'"

TECHNICAL COMPOSITION:
- Triangular composition: Mother at apex, two Harukas at base corners
- Vertical symmetry with asymmetrical lighting (left warm, right cool)
- Central focus: The fusion/merge point where the two Harukas meet
- Depth: Mother appears behind/enveloping the two forms, creating a "container" effect
- Scale: All three figures equally prominent; no visual hierarchy
- Aspect ratio: Wide horizontal (16:9) to accommodate the duality, or tall vertical (9:16) for emotional weight

COLOR PALETTE PROGRESSION:
```

Bright Haruka:      Sakura pink(#FFB7D9) + Cyan(#7FD4E1) + Gold highlights

```
LIGHTING APPROACH:
- Hanako's light: Warm, diffuse, from within (suggesting unconditional acceptance)
- Bright Haruka's light: Golden-white, structured (representing the public persona's rules)
- Shadow Haruka's light: Cool, ethereal, suggesting depth and mystery
```

## 附錄：Prompt 生成最佳實踐

### 對 Midjourney 最有效的提示詞結構

1. **開場描述** (5-10%)：場景類型 + 主要角色
2. **視覺細節** (30-40%)：衣著、髮型、表情、姿態
3. **環境設置** (20-25%)：背景、建築、自然元素
4. **光線與氣氛** (15-20%)：光源、色溫、情緒
5. **技術規格** (10-15%)：構圖、比例、渲染風格
6. **參考與風格** (5-10%)：參考藝術家、視覺風格

### 色彩代碼應用

所有 HEX 代碼均可直接用於：
- Midjourney：直接在 prompt 中寫 `hexadecimal color #XXXXXX`
- Photoshop：複製代碼 → 貼入色彩選擇器
- Figma：複製代碼 → 貼入顏色欄
- 其他工具：大多支持 HEX 代碼輸入

### 特殊效果描述

- **Subsurface scattering**（次表面散射）：用於皮膚、薄紙、晶體的內在發光
- **Chromatic aberration**（色差）：現實扭曲、時間線撕裂視覺化
- **Volumetric lighting**（體積光）：通過霧霾或塵埃看到光束
- **Depth of field**（景深）：主體清晰、背景模糊，創造焦點
- **Vignette**（暗角）：邊緣逐漸變暗，強調中心人物
