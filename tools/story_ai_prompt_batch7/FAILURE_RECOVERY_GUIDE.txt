FAILURE RECOVERY GUIDE

Problem 1 — The other AI starts making canon decisions on its own
What you do:
- stop it
- remind it that contradictions / duplicates / atom finalization / canonization require author approval
- send it back to Author Gate
Useful command:
"Stop. You are making an unauthorized decision. Move this item into QUESTION_QUEUE.md and ask me in A/B/C format first."

Problem 2 — It jumps from story room straight to write-back
What you do:
- stop it
- ask for phase verification
Useful command:
"Do not jump phases. Verify entry criteria, exit criteria, blockers, and handoff summary before any write-back."

Problem 3 — It forgets what was done before
What you do:
- invoke resume / recovery
Useful command:
"Do not continue from memory. Read SESSION_LEDGER.md, NEXT_ACTION.md, and relevant registries first, then restate current phase and next safe action."

Problem 4 — It says 'done' but no ledger updated
What you do:
- reject completion
Useful command:
"This phase is not complete. Update SESSION_LEDGER.md and NEXT_ACTION.md, then restate blockers and handoff status."

Problem 5 — It hallucinates source facts
What you do:
- force traceability check
Useful command:
"Do not state this as canon truth without DocID, SectionID, Raw Quote, or an explicit author decision."

Problem 6 — It produces too much duplicated content
What you do:
- route it to duplicate review
Useful command:
"Do not keep parallel ownership. Identify the single responsible file, convert all others to mention/link only, and ask me before any meaning-changing dedupe."

Problem 7 — It asks too many chaotic questions
What you do:
- force batching and prioritization
Useful command:
"Rebatch your questions. Ask only the highest-impact blockers first, in A/B/C format, with impact notes."

Problem 8 — It mixes proposal with canon
What you do:
- force label hygiene
Useful command:
"Separate [PROPOSAL], [INFER], and canon-confirmed content. Do not merge them."
