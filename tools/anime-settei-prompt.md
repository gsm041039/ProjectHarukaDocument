# Fixed Anime Settei Prompt Block

每次生成角色 settei／model sheet 變體時，必須逐字包含以下 block。角色專屬修改指示可以放在 block 前後，但不可刪減、改寫或縮短本 block。

```text
/////////////////////anime settei sheet/////////////////////
official anime settei sheet, professional TV-anime production model sheet, simplified character turnaround sheet, one single character only, clean technical reference page, 2D anime production design document

use the reference image as the primary source of character identity and costume concept, but redraw it at a much simpler TV-animation production detail level. preserve the same character identity, same face and eye style, same hairstyle silhouette, same main outfit concept, same motif family, same main color blocking, same role and overall design family. do not redesign the character into a different costume.

TARGET DETAIL LEVEL:
clean official TV anime settei sheet, medium-low detail density, production-safe, easy to redraw, broad readable shapes, large clean costume areas, simple silhouette, clear color blocking, limited garment layers, limited decoration, no high-detail concept-art density.

DETAIL BUDGET RULE:
the final design must have around 40% to 55% of the reference image’s original drawn detail density. preserve only the largest and most recognizable design decisions. simplify or merge small parts into larger cleaner shapes. every costume area should be readable from a distance. do not fill empty areas with extra decoration.

LARGE SHAPE FIRST RULE:
keep large shapes large. do not break one large costume panel into many small pieces. do not over-segment skirts, sleeves, capes, ribbons, belts, straps, or cloth tails. use big simple panels and clear silhouettes. leave some calm empty cloth areas with no decoration.

DECORATION CONTROL:
details may appear only in 3 to 5 focal zones maximum, such as chest emblem, collar, waist, hair accessory, cuffs, shoes, or one signature accessory area. all other areas must remain clean and quiet. do not decorate every edge. do not add new unnecessary ornaments.

REPEATED ELEMENT LIMIT:
if the design includes chains, straps, ribbons, feathers, lace, spikes, charms, crosses, stitches, trims, gems, beads, broken cloth tips, or repeated motifs, reduce them to a few large representative elements only.
maximum chains: 1 to 2 short simple chain segments.
maximum dangling ornaments: 2 to 4 total.
maximum skirt pleat lines: 4 to 6 large simple fold lines total.
maximum cloth split tips: 3 to 5 large tips total.
maximum small symbols or motifs: 3 to 5 total.
no dense repeated patterns, no chain curtains, no many link clusters, no many tiny straps, no many small hanging pieces.

CLOTH SIMPLIFICATION:
skirts, sleeves, cape panels, draped fabric, ribbons, and layered garments must use broad simple cloth shapes with only a few major folds. folds should be clean, graphic, and animation-friendly. avoid micro-folds, dense wrinkles, many pleats, many crease lines, tiny torn edges, and complex cloth texture.

EDGE SIMPLIFICATION:
if the original design has ragged hems, broken edges, cloud-shaped edges, asymmetrical cloth, layered skirts, or decorative cloth tails, keep the motif but simplify it into fewer larger cleaner shapes. keep important cloud-shaped clothing edges if they are part of the original design, but make them broad, readable, and not overly detailed.

LINEART AND COLOR:
clean neat anime lineart, clear outlines, minimal internal linework, flat colors, extremely flat cel shading, very minimal 1-2 tone shading only, hard-edged shadow shapes only, no soft rendering, no painterly rendering, no fabric sheen, no glossy material rendering, no gradients, no bloom, no rim light, no atmospheric lighting.

SHEET LAYOUT:
full body front view and full body back view, strict A-pose, neutral standing pose, large main figures on the left. on the right include expression heads, hairstyle callouts, accessory callouts, costume breakdown, close-up parts, shoe detail, hand/glove detail if needed, and color palette swatches. simple light grey background, Traditional Chinese text annotations, organized rectangular layout, clean spacing, clear hierarchy, professional anime settei sheet style.

the final result should look like the same character and same costume concept, but simplified into a clean, production-safe official TV anime model sheet.
```
