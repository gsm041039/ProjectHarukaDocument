CONSEQUENCE-DRIVEN PROGRESSION PATCH
Supplemental Rule for Story Room and Beat Sheet Review
======================================================

RULE NAME
---------
Consequence-Driven Progression Law (CDP Law)

CORE PROHIBITION
----------------
Plot progression must NOT be driven solely by a character's internal decision or sudden motivation shift.

FORBIDDEN construction:
"Character X suddenly felt Y, so they decided to do Z."

REQUIRED construction:
"External event E occurred (combat damage / resource failure / environmental collapse / random encounter).
Character X was forced to react.
In the course of reacting, they made decision Z, which had consequence R on relationships."

FOUR-BEAT SCENE TEMPLATE (MANDATORY)
-------------------------------------
Every scene / beat must be checkable against:

1. Cost Signal
   External random event that forces a change of state.
   Examples: weapon destroyed, blood glucose alarm triggers, building collapses, unexpected civilian encounter.

2. Instinctive Reaction
   Character's immediate survival-level response.
   Not yet a choice — a reflex.

3. Undertaking Action
   The plan that shifts because of the Cost Signal.
   This is the forced re-routing, not the original intention.

4. Relational Consequence
   What changes in relationship, trust, secret-sharing, or emotional exposure
   as a direct result of the forced re-routing.

RISK TAGGING PROTOCOL
----------------------
If a beat has no identifiable Cost Signal:
- Tag it: [RISK: INTENTION-DRIVEN]
- Do not silently accept it.
- Flag it in Questions for Author.

VALID EXCEPTION
---------------
Internal transformation moments are permitted IF:
- They are precipitated by a prior external Cost Signal.
- The internal shift is a REACTION to physical/environmental forcing, not a spontaneous decision.

EXAMPLE — FORBIDDEN
--------------------
"Haruka felt sorry for Zhuyin, so she went into the underground zone to find her."

EXAMPLE — REQUIRED
------------------
"Haruka's glucose monitor was destroyed in combat. She needed a replacement urgently.
Forced into the night district black market, she randomly encountered Zhuyin mid-withdrawal, being hunted.
To escape, both were forced into temporary cooperation."

SCOPE
-----
This rule applies to:
- Beat Sheet generation and review
- Scene outline review
- Story Room propulsion monitoring (axis 13)
- Any AI-generated plot suggestion

This rule does NOT apply to:
- Theme discussion
- Character profile development
- World-building
