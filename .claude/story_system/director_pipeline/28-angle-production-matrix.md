# 28-Angle Story / Dialogue / Game Direction Production Matrix

呢份矩陣係 reasoning coverage map，唔係每次都要輸出 28 段。`story-work-readiness-diagnostician` 同 `/story-orchestrator` 應按 deliverable、風險、重要性同 gameplay 複雜度選取相關角度。

## A. Narrative Truth
1. Canon / source authority
2. Timeline and continuity
3. Scene function in sequence / act
4. Setup, payoff and consequence
5. Theme / moral pressure

## B. Character Truth
6. Identity, history and values
7. Character arc position
8. Want, need, fear, shame and wound
9. Defence mechanism / coping strategy
10. Current physical and emotional residue
11. Knowledge, belief, secret and misunderstanding
12. Relationship history, trust and dependency
13. Status and multi-axis power
14. Character voice and listener-specific variants

## C. Dramatic Construction
15. Objective, obstacle, stakes and urgency
16. Beat progression and tactic changes
17. Subtext, silence and action substitution
18. Information architecture and reveal timing
19. Turn, reversal, decision and landing
20. Ensemble focus, overlap and reaction

## D. Game Direction
21. Gameplay objective and narrative alignment
22. Player agency / control / camera ownership
23. Combat readability and mechanic-character expression
24. Trigger, interruption, fallback, retry and state validity
25. Spatial design, blocking, location and environmental storytelling
26. Camera, editing, montage, audio and performance integration

## E. Production Readiness
27. VO, localization, subtitles, accessibility and asset feasibility
28. Implementation schema, stable IDs, QA, playtest, versioning and downstream continuity

## Scale Rule
- Low-risk optional bark: 6–10 relevant angles.
- Standard gameplay scene: 12–18 relevant angles.
- Major character / boss / irreversible scene: 20–28 relevant angles plus cross-discipline room.

每個被選角度應有：source/evidence、decision、risk、owner、status。
