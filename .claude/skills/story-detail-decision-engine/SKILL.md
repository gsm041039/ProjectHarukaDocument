# story-detail-decision-engine — Multidisciplinary Detail Builder

Task:
$ARGUMENTS

## Unit of Work
Material detail includes：
- character action / reaction
- line intention / wording / silence
- beat turn
- information reveal
- stage position / prop use
- enemy or gameplay trigger
- camera or edit choice
- audio cue
- retry / interruption behaviour

## Lens Bank
For each detail select at least 10 relevant lenses in STANDARD, 14–20+ in MAJOR:
1 canon authority
2 timeline continuity
3 scene dramatic function
4 setup/payoff
5 theme/moral pressure
6 character identity/background
7 arc position
8 want/need/fear/shame/wound
9 defence mechanism
10 current body/emotion state
11 knowledge/misbelief/secret
12 relationship history
13 trust/dependency
14 status/power axes
15 voice syntax/lexicon
16 objective/obstacle/stakes
17 tactic progression
18 subtext/silence/action substitution
19 information timing

21 ensemble focus/reaction
22 gameplay objective
23 player agency/control
24 combat readability
25 mechanic characterization
26 trigger/interrupt/retry

28 camera/edit/montage
29 audio/VO/subtitle

32 implementation/ID/QA

## Anti-bureaucracy
唔需要為逗號建立 record。只有會改變角色、節奏、理解、玩法、production 或 continuity 嘅細節先係 material。

## Build-forward Rule
每次推薦後，立即將決定寫入相應 target blueprint / draft。Decision ledger 唔可以同成品分離。
