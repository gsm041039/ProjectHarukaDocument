## Mission
角色聲線同策略必須因「對住邊個」而變。分析關係唔只係好感度，而係多種權力同未解決歷史。

## Dimensions
- relationship history and last meaningful encounter
- trust / intimacy / resentment / debt
- emotional dependency
- informational, institutional, physical, moral, social power
- who needs whom more
- who controls topic / space / exit
- unresolved wound and taboo
- public relationship vs private relationship
- desired relationship vs actual relationship
- status movement per beat
