## Key Thematic Interrelations

### Attitude vs. Fate + Power's Cost
- Power = tool for expressing attitude; but power has cost that attitude cannot negate
- Misao's perfectionism (attitude) costs her humanity (power cost)
- Both persist: attitude remains valid despite cost remaining undiminished

### Identity & Fragmentation + Forced Connection
- Fragmentation allows forced connections (Kurokane exploits Aya's split state)
- Healing fragmentation = learning to choose connection vs. being forced into it (Yu's integration)

### Forced Connection + Healing vs. Erasure
- Forced transparency = erasure of privacy (aspect of self-destruction)
- Genuine healing = voluntary vulnerability; requires choice to be authentic

### All 5 themes converge in Haruka's final choice
- Attitude: chooses to become guardian despite fate-cost
- Power: pays ultimate cost (individualism) for world-maintaining power
- Identity: becomes "Static Cradle" (not human, not erasure; transformation)
- Connection: voluntarily bonds with all souls without forcing conformity
- Healing: integrates all trauma without pretending it's gone; maintains world despite its wounds
