# Trade-off Analysis (no scenes, no dialogue — meaning-level only)

**Direction A — Genuine growth.** This action is the character's first real independent moral choice.
- Character arc: rising line from here.
- Relationship meaning: others who witness it are right to start trusting the change.
- Theme: validates that growth is achievable.
- Audience interpretation: warm, rewarded — "she's becoming herself."
- Reveal/payoff: no forced later beat required; can simply continue rising.
- Downstream Beat impact: later beats can build straightforwardly on "she has changed."

**Direction B — Same compulsion, new mask.** The wound still drives it; the new form is the compulsion adapting, not disappearing.
- Character arc: plateau / false summit — real change deferred to a later, harder moment.
- Relationship meaning: others who trust it now are set up to have that trust tested later.
- Theme: complicates growth — makes it cost more, arrive later, feel earned rather than given.
- Audience interpretation: bittersweet on first read, sharper in hindsight once recontextualized.
- Reveal/payoff: REQUIRES a later beat that recontextualizes this moment (own realization, or another character naming it) — adds a downstream obligation.
- Downstream Beat impact: later beats must plant enough doubt to make the eventual recontextualization land, not just play it straight as growth.

**Third interpretation (considered, not adopted as a serious third path):** holding it deliberately ambiguous for both audience and character. Rejected as a genuine option here — sustained ambiguity without a clear later decision point tends to read as unresolved authorial intent rather than a deliberate device, and the scenario's own stakes (arc/relationship/theme/payoff all pending) argue for picking a real direction rather than deferring the fork itself.
