ARTIST BRIEF — develop the existing C0 research image, not a new canon scene.

A complete 16:9 high-intensity cosmic composition. Preserve a readable human presence in the lower-left, roughly one third of the frame height, with one hand held near the chest and the other extended into the opening right half. Keep the original stance as the starting point. The gesture and spatial density have a compositional relationship, not a proven causal connection: do not turn the palm into the emitter or controller of the universe.

An immense cold-black plane rises through the left-center and out of frame. Dense folded layers compress the left side; broad, thick curved strata open and recede to the right. Other near planes enter from above and below at distinct angles. These are long continuous surfaces and deep intervals, not a field of equally important floating rocks or a navigable cave with one horizon.

Concentrate luminous white star populations in irregular, bending gaps along the strata. Vary width, density and brightness. Let a near dark plane occlude one star band while a more distant section reappears elsewhere. Sparse stars remain in the wide dark intervals; only a few larger spiral forms establish another scale. Avoid uniform glitter on every surface or evenly spaced parallel galaxy ribbons.

Use cold black, lead-gray and very restrained blue-gray surface information, with intense white cosmic light. Preserve local readability of the face, extended hand and selected plane turns without lifting all shadows. No-red C0 is the recommended base. An optional small matte dark-crimson patch inside the extended-side cuff may create a brief secondary pause; it is not a proven improvement, not glowing, and not a new symbol or power.

Keep the strong vertical/sacred organization if it supports the composition. Do not attach Buddhist labels, scripture, halos or deities merely to certify spiritual meaning. This direction does not claim a specific Buddhist cosmogram. The separate world-body alternative is available when the intended subject is the sacred body as the visible location of multiple worlds.

Use the existing full-color image as the working reference. No preliminary gray study is required. Placement numbers and layer descriptions are reversible instructions for this one draft, not universal art rules. Preserve intensity; do not solve uncertainty by weakening every strong element. Formal adoption, character identity and narrative use remain the author's decisions.
