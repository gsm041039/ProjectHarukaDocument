# Cosmic stage depth section — v4 minimum deliverable

Files: `cosmos_depth_section.svg` (native vector, existing L00–L14 content IDs plus annotations) and `cosmos_depth_section.png` (rendered inspection copy).

## What this establishes
A proposed side elevation and two local construction details for the existing high-intensity cosmic candidate. This fills the missing side-section/exploded-construction slot only. It does not establish a tested 3D scene, final compositing, segmentation, a camera range, canon cosmology, or an engine/performance pass. Fifteen editable content groups are not fifteen meshes or draw calls.

Visible evidence was checked in v03 `cosmos_B31_near_clusters.png` and `cosmos_BE_material_exposure.png`: human hand contact and a load-bearing foreground, immense inverted structure/fissure, clearly expressed perimeter thickness, uneven star clusters and dark corridors. Exact hidden geometry, an independent second tier, and all coordinates below are hypotheses. The v03 construction SVG is a drawn reconstruction, not recovered masks.

Star dots in the section are sparse location symbols, not a medium-intensity or no-star alternate. Production target remains the high-intensity source with dense, unequal clusters, strong size variation and dark corridors. Three depth families do not mean three full-screen transparent cards.
